# Worked, verified examples

## Rainbow bridge (plate -> toggle mirror -> walkable beam across a pit). 1 ghost.
Emitter at (12,8) fires DOWN. Toggle mirror `A` (group 0) sits at (12,14) flush in the floor.
At rest `A` is `/`, so a downward beam turns LEFT and is swallowed by the floor tile at (11,14) —
harmless. While plate `!` at (6,14) is held, the mirror becomes `\` and the beam turns RIGHT,
running along row 14 across the pit (cols 13..23) and terminating on the floor at col 24.
Because the bridge is in the SAME row as the floor, you walk off the ledge, drop 8px onto the
rainbow, cross, and step-assist lifts you back onto the far ledge.

(x=104 is the plate at col 6: 6*16+8. x=408 is the castle at col 25.)

## Prism feeding a receiver that opens a gate. 2 ghosts.
The beam is turned on by plate `!` (group 0 mirror `A` at (7,14)); it runs along row 14, hits the
prism `p` at (13,14). RED turns left (UP col 13), GREEN goes straight (a second bridge along row
14), BLUE turns right (DOWN, swallowed by the rock below). The red band is walked up col 13 into
a fixed `\` at (13,6) which sends it LEFT along row 6 into toggle mirror `D` (group 1) at (6,6);
holding plate `@` turns that mirror so red goes UP into receiver `R` at (6,2), which powers the

## Gotchas learned the hard way
- A gate must be **4 tiles tall** (rows r..r+3) or the player jumps over it (jump clears ~49px).
- A toggle mirror's REST orientation must fire into rock or off-screen. If the rest state already
  solves the level there is no puzzle.
- Put plates **in the floor row**, replacing a `#`, so they sit flush and can be stood on.
- A beam that runs one row ABOVE a floor is 8px too high to step onto, AND every unicorn standing
  on that floor eclipses it. Either is usually a bug. Route bridges in the floor's own row.
- `C` is the castle. Do not use `C` for cyan; the cyan receiver is `K` and the cyan filter is `k`.
- Beams stop at receivers, walls, plates, emitters and closed doors. They pass through clouds,
  thorns, the goal, sockets and open doors.
